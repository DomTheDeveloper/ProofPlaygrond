# Prime representations, prime intervals and two-generator densities

| Material | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Prime averages | The earliest reorderable block of 230 consecutive primes in Rivera’s puzzle begins at 30,271. The certificate checks the witness and minimality. The all-length conclusion invokes Shiu’s theorem. |
| Quadratic forms | The overlap note derives the spectrum for two primitive positive-definite binary quadratic forms. The catalogue study enumerates congruence rules and full prime sets under the analytic assumptions stated there and credits the Clark–Hicks–Parshall–Thompson catalogue. |
| Prime-bit windows | Exhaustive counts for 32-bit odd integers give the sharp guarantee when eight consecutive interior bits are free. The verifier and counterexamples are in `prime-window/` in the archive. |
| Prime intervals | The prescribed-last-digit result between successive cubes uses Dirichlet GRH for its tail. The consecutive-real-power paper states its explicit RH hypothesis. Each write-up distinguishes its finite checks from its analytic input. |
| Erdős 488 | The paper proves the sharp ratio and unique extremizer for exactly two generators with fixed maximum. |
| The middle-third Cantor set | The Lean file establishes uncountably many binary-normal Liouville numbers in that set. |
