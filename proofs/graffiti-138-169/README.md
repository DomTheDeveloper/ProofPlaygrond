# Written on the Wall II, conjectures 138 and 169

[Ermelinda DeLaViña’s source catalogue](https://www.uhd.edu/academics/sciences/mathematics-statistics/wowii/files/conjectures.json)

**138.** A 19-vertex tree violates the proposed lower bound on the number of vertices in a longest induced path. The accompanying paper gives the graph family, the invariants and the catalogue convention for a unique maximum independent set. `WOWII138.lean` contains the finite certificate.

**169.** Every finite connected graph with at least two vertices has a spanning tree with at least one plus the difference between the largest and smallest even-distance counts in the original graph. With the degree-one definition of leaf, the singleton is the unique exception. The audit directory documents the prior literature and the exact statement checked in Lean 4.33.1. To replay the audit, put `Graffiti169.lean` and `Audit169.lean` in the same Lean search path.

These are submissions for mathematical review. The catalogue supplied the conjectures. The verification materials were developed with AI assistance.
